Privacy Policy

Adam personal listing tool

Last updated: 7 November 2026

What this tool is

This is a private tool used by one individual, its developer, to manage his own eBay listings through eBay's official APIs. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What data it handles

  • It acts only on the developer's own eBay account, with permission granted by him through eBay's sign-in and consent page.
  • It does not collect, store or share any other eBay user's personal information, user IDs or passwords.
  • It never sees or stores the developer's eBay password.
  • Access tokens are kept privately on the developer's own computer and are not shared.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or passed to third parties.

Deletion

Access can be withdrawn at any time by revoking the application's access in the eBay account settings.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the GitHub profile that hosts this page.
